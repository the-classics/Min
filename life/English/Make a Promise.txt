Pinky promise / pinky swear: A playful, often childlike promise sealed by hooking little fingers.
I promise: A straightforward, everyday way to say you’re making a promise.
It’s a deal / Deal?: A casual way to confirm an agreement—roughly, “We’re agreed.”

Pinky promise / pinky swear — 拉钩约定；勾小指发誓
I promise — 我保证；我答应
It’s a deal / Deal? — 说定了；成交？
